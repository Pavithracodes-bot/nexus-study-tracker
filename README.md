**NEXUS** is a gamified, distraction-free study dashboard designed to help students stay focused and track their academic progress. It combines productivity tools with game mechanics (XP, Coins, Levels) to make studying engaging.

🚀 **Live Demo:** 🚀 Live Demo: [https://pavithracodes-bot.github.io/nexus-study-tracker/ ]


## ✨ Key Features

### 🧠 **Focus Core**
- **Immersive Timer:** 3D animated countdown ring with tilt effects.
- **Neuro-Ambience:** Selectable background noise (White, Pink, Brown, Silent) to boost concentration.
- **Focus Guard:** Detects inactivity and nudges you back to work.

### 📊 **Academics & Analytics**
- **Syllabus Tracker:** Pre-loaded NCERT checklists for Class 11 & 12 (Physics, Chem, Math, Bio, CS).
- **Visual Analytics:** Interactive bar charts and progress rings to visualize mastery.
- **Daily Reports:** Generate and download text reports of your study sessions.

### 🎮 **Gamification**
- **XP & Leveling System:** Earn XP for finishing tasks and study sessions.
- **Reward Shop:** Use coins to buy new Room Themes (Cyber, Rain, Library) and Desk Items.
- **Daily Missions:** Complete quests to maintain your streak.

### 📱 **Technical Highlights**
- **Fully Responsive:** Optimized for Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop.
- **Privacy First:** All data is saved locally in your browser (`localStorage`). No servers, no tracking.
- **Lightweight:** Built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript.
